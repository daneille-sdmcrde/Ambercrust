Ambercrust is a specialty pastry café opening in Dologon, Maramag, Bukidnon. We bring together expertly crafted espresso drinks, single-origin coffee, and locally sourced pastries in a welcoming neighborhood space.

More than a café, Ambercrust is a place for students, professionals, and neighbors to build relationships, find a little room to focus, and enjoy good food together.

YOUR SAFETY MATTERS

Know what's in your food.

We believe customers should be able to make informed choices. Please review the allergen information and speak with our staff before ordering if you have a food allergy.

Wheat / Gluten Commonly found in croissants, cinnamon rolls, banana bread, and cookies.
Milk / Dairy May be present in butter pastries, chocolate products, and milk-based coffee.
Eggs May be used in pastry and baked-good recipes.
Soy May be present in chocolate, certain ingredients, or alternative milk products.
Nuts Not listed as an intentional ingredient in this sample menu. Cross-contact must still be checked.
Important allergen notice

Menu allergen details are preliminary and must be confirmed against the actual recipes, ingredient labels, and supplier information. Products may be prepared or handled in shared areas where cross-contact can occur. We cannot guarantee that any item is allergen-free. Please inform our staff about your allergy before placing an order.

Prices are proposed sample prices in Philippine pesos (₱) and may be adjusted based on actual ingredient and operating costs. Menu ingredients and allergen details must be verified before serving.
